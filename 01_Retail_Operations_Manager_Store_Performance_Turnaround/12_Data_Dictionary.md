# Data Dictionary

| Metric | Definition / Formula | Unit | Direction | Owner | Refresh |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sales | Net sales for reporting period | $ | Higher contextual | Store Ops | Weekly |
| Transactions | Completed customer transactions | Count | Higher contextual | Store Ops | Weekly |
| Traffic | Store visits/entries | Count | Contextual | Store Ops | Weekly |
| Conversion Rate | Transactions / Traffic | % | Higher | Store Ops | Weekly |
| Avg Transaction Value | Sales / Transactions | $ | Higher contextual | Store Ops | Weekly |
| Labor Cost % | Labor cost / Sales | % | Lower with guardrails | Store Ops | Weekly |
| NPS | Promoters % minus Detractors % | Score | Higher | CX Lead | Weekly |
| Avg Wait Min | Mean measured checkout wait | Minutes | Lower | Front End | Weekly |
| Shrink % | Inventory loss / relevant sales or inventory basis | % | Lower | Inventory | Weekly |
| Schedule Adherence % | Worked as scheduled / scheduled requirement | % | Higher | Store Ops | Weekly |
| Task Completion % | Completed required tasks / required tasks | % | Higher | Assistant Mgr | Weekly |

## Data Quality Rules
One reporting calendar; documented unit definitions; no silent formula changes; missing data flagged; outliers investigated; source extracts retained where applicable.
