# Standard Operating Procedures

## SOP 1 — Daily Opening Readiness
Owner: Assistant Manager. Frequency: Daily.
1. Verify scheduled leadership and critical coverage.
2. Review call-outs and coverage response.
3. Confirm front-end/register readiness.
4. Review priority tasks and owners.
5. Review high-risk inventory/receiving items.
6. Record exceptions and escalate unresolved critical issues.
Evidence: completed opening checklist.

## SOP 2 — Labor Variance Review
Owner: Store Operations Lead. Frequency: Daily.
1. Compare planned vs actual hours.
2. Identify variance >3%.
3. Separate demand variance, attendance variance and management adjustment.
4. Correct same-day/next-day deployment where possible.
5. Record material decisions.

## SOP 3 — Queue Escalation
Owner: Front End Lead. Frequency: Continuous.
1. Monitor queue.
2. At >4 customers or >5-minute estimated wait, activate backup.
3. Reassess after intervention.
4. Log repeated threshold events.
5. Escalate capacity pattern in weekly review.

## SOP 4 — Weekly Cycle Count
Owner: Inventory Lead. Frequency: Weekly.
Follow the eight-step cycle-count procedure in the inventory control plan. Variance >1.5% requires investigation.

## SOP 5 — KPI Data Validation
Owner: Project Lead. Frequency: Weekly.
1. Confirm reporting period.
2. Reconcile totals to source extracts.
3. Check missing/duplicate records.
4. Validate formulas and units.
5. Compare large week-over-week changes.
6. Resolve critical issues before publishing.

## SOP 6 — Action Aging
Owner: Project Lead. Frequency: Weekly.
Review all open actions. Any action >7 days overdue is escalated unless a revised date and rationale are approved.
