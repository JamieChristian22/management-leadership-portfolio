# Weekly Operating Review

## Purpose
Convert KPI reporting into decisions, accountability and corrective action.

## Participants
Store Manager, Store Operations Lead, Assistant Manager, Front End Lead, Inventory Lead, Project Lead. District Manager joins for escalations or monthly review.

## 45-Minute Agenda
**0–5 min:** decisions/actions from prior week.
**5–15 min:** executive KPI scorecard and variance.
**15–25 min:** workstream root causes.
**25–33 min:** risks, controls and dependencies.
**33–40 min:** decisions and resource tradeoffs.
**40–45 min:** confirm owners, due dates and escalations.

## Required Pre-Read
Dashboard refreshed and validated; action plan current; high/medium risks updated; exceptions summarized; decisions requiring sponsor input identified.

## Decision Standard
A red KPI must leave the meeting with an owner, corrective action, due date and measurable recovery condition.

## Meeting Output
Updated action plan, risk register, decision log and any approved control changes.
