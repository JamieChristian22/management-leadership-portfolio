# Change Management & Communications Plan

## Change Objective
Move new controls from project activity to normal management behavior without creating unnecessary administrative burden.

## Stakeholder Messages
**Store leadership:** why the turnaround matters, decision guardrails, accountability.
**Workstream leads:** control ownership, evidence expectations, escalation rules.
**Associates:** what changes in daily work, why it improves operations/customer experience, where to raise obstacles.
**District leadership:** trend, risk, decisions needed and sustainability.

## Channels & Cadence
Daily huddles for immediate execution; weekly operating review for decisions; biweekly sponsor update for tradeoffs; monthly district summary for oversight.

## Adoption Measures
Checklist completion, action closure, schedule adherence, control exceptions, attendance at reviews and percentage of controls operating without project-lead intervention.

## Resistance Handling
Identify the specific friction, distinguish capacity from understanding, simplify controls that do not add decision value, retrain where needed, and escalate persistent non-compliance through normal leadership channels.
