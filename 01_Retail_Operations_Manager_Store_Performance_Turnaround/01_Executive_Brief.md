# Executive Brief

## Executive Situation
The simulated store enters the turnaround with performance deterioration across labor, customer experience, inventory control and execution. The indicators are interdependent: weak schedule adherence produces coverage gaps; coverage gaps increase queue time; service friction lowers NPS; inconsistent routines weaken task completion and inventory discipline.

## Baseline and Target
| KPI | Baseline | 12-Week Target | Management Intent |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Labor Cost % | 18.9% | 16.8% | Improve deployment, not indiscriminate hour cuts |
| NPS | 52 | 66+ | Recover advocacy through service consistency |
| Avg Wait Time | 8.4 min | 5.5 min | Trigger backup coverage before queues compound |
| Shrink | 3.8% | 2.6% | Strengthen counts, receiving and exception ownership |
| Schedule Adherence | 74% | 90%+ | Improve reliability of planned coverage |
| Task Completion | 71% | 93%+ | Create visible ownership and closed-loop follow-up |

## Strategic Priorities
**1. Stabilize execution.** Introduce opening/closing controls, clear ownership and same-day exception escalation.

**2. Match labor to demand.** Use traffic and transaction patterns to protect peak periods while removing low-value overlap.

**3. Protect the customer.** Establish queue thresholds, backup-register triggers and NPS root-cause review.

**4. Tighten inventory control.** Increase cycle-count discipline and investigate high-risk SKU/receiving exceptions.

**5. Establish governance.** Use one weekly operating review with a single dashboard, action log and risk register.

## Decision Guardrails
Labor savings are not accepted if wait time or NPS materially deteriorates. KPI improvement must be sustained, not produced by one-time interventions. High risks require named owners and mitigation dates. Data quality issues are resolved before executive readout.

## Modeled Outcome
By Week 12, the model reaches labor cost 16.7%, NPS 67, wait time 5.3 minutes, shrink 2.5%, schedule adherence 91% and task completion 95%. These are simulated scenario results used to test the operating model.
