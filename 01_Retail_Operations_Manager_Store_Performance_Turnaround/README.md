# Retail Operations Management — Store Performance Turnaround

## Purpose
A corporate-style simulated operating turnaround for a multi-department retail store. The work demonstrates diagnosis, governance, KPI management, labor optimization, customer-experience controls, shrink reduction, execution discipline, and sustainment.

> **Scenario disclosure:** All company, store, employee, operational and financial data in this project are simulated. Modeled outcomes demonstrate the management approach and are not claims of results achieved for an employer.

## Scenario
Leadership identified six connected issues: labor cost above plan, weak schedule adherence, long checkout wait times, low customer advocacy, elevated inventory shrink, and inconsistent task completion. The turnaround uses a 12-week operating cadence to stabilize controls, correct root causes, track leading/lagging indicators, and transition successful controls into business-as-usual ownership.

## Business Objectives
- Reduce labor cost from 18.9% to 16.8% without degrading service.
- Increase NPS from 52 to 66+.
- Reduce average wait time from 8.4 to 5.5 minutes or better.
- Reduce shrink from 3.8% to 2.6% or better.
- Increase schedule adherence from 74% to 90%+.
- Increase task completion from 71% to 93%+.
- Establish auditable controls, escalation thresholds, owners and governance.

## Management Method
**Diagnose → Prioritize → Assign → Execute → Measure → Correct → Standardize → Sustain**

The workbook is the operational source of truth. Documentation defines how decisions are made and how controls are executed.

## Executive Operating Review Upgrade

The operating workbook includes an executive management layer designed for a corporate weekly business review. It provides a direction-aware KPI scorecard, target variance, attainment, trend versus baseline, health status, executive commentary, management priorities, early-warning thresholds, a 30-day control plan, and 12-week trend charts.

The management readout is intentionally action-oriented: what happened, why it happened, what the team is doing, what decision is required, who owns the response, and how sustainment will be tested.

All performance data and outcomes in this project are simulated/modelled for demonstration purposes.
