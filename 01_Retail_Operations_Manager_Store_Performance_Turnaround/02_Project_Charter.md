# Project Charter

## Project
Retail Store Performance Turnaround — 12-Week Stabilization and Sustainment

## Objective
Restore predictable store performance by improving labor deployment, customer experience, inventory controls, schedule adherence and task execution while creating a repeatable management system.

## In Scope
Store labor planning; schedule adherence; queue management; NPS review; opening/closing controls; inventory cycle counts; KPI reporting; risk/action governance; leadership cadence; sustainment.

## Out of Scope
Major capital projects; store remodels; enterprise pricing strategy; merchandising assortment redesign; vendor contract renegotiation; workforce reductions; system replacement.

## Success Criteria
Targets are considered achieved only when the KPI meets target and the supporting control is operational. No critical unresolved data-quality issue may exist at close. All BAU controls require an owner, frequency, evidence standard and escalation path.

## Governance
- Sponsor: Store Manager
- Workstream owners: Store Operations, Front End, Inventory, Assistant Manager
- Weekly operating review: KPI variance, actions, risks, decisions
- Biweekly sponsor review: escalations, tradeoffs, resource decisions
- Monthly district readout: sustained trend and material risks

## Timeline
Weeks 1–2: baseline validation and stabilization.
Weeks 3–6: intervention deployment and early correction.
Weeks 7–10: optimization and control hardening.
Weeks 11–12: sustainment, handoff and close.

## Assumptions
Traffic and transaction data are sufficiently reliable for daypart planning. Leaders have authority to reallocate coverage within approved labor. Existing systems can produce the required KPI extracts.

## Constraints
No incremental headcount assumption. Interventions must fit normal store operating rhythms. Service and safety controls cannot be compromised for cost.
