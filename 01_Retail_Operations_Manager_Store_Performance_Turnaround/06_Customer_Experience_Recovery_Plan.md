# Customer Experience Recovery Plan

## Objectives
Reduce average wait from 8.4 to 5.5 minutes or better and improve NPS from 52 to 66+.

## Queue Control
Trigger backup coverage when either threshold is reached: more than four customers waiting or estimated wait above five minutes. Front End Lead owns activation and records material exceptions.

## NPS Root-Cause Review
Classify comments into wait time, associate interaction, availability, checkout accuracy, store condition and other. Identify recurring themes by week and connect them to operational actions.

## Service Recovery
Acknowledge the issue, resolve what can be resolved immediately, document repeatable failure modes and escalate systemic causes. Individual recovery does not replace root-cause correction.

## Leading Indicators
Queue threshold events, backup response time, register availability and staffing adherence.

## Lagging Indicators
Average wait time and NPS.

## Guardrail
Labor efficiency actions are reversed or modified if customer indicators deteriorate beyond agreed thresholds.
