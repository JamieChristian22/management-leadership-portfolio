# Labor Optimization Plan

## Goal
Move labor cost from 18.9% toward 16.8% while protecting wait time and NPS.

## Method
1. Segment traffic and transactions by daypart.
2. Calculate conversion, transactions per labor hour and sales per labor hour.
3. Identify over-covered low-demand periods and under-covered peaks.
4. Reallocate hours before removing hours.
5. Protect critical opening, closing, receiving and peak front-end coverage.
6. Review scheduled vs actual hours daily.
7. Escalate unfavorable labor variance >3%.

## Staffing Decision Rules
- Add/shift coverage when queue exceeds 4 customers or 5 minutes.
- Do not reduce coverage in a daypart with worsening NPS and wait time.
- Use cross-training before overtime where operationally feasible.
- Investigate repeated call-outs separately from demand-planning errors.
- Validate savings over multiple weeks.

## Weekly Analysis
Compare planned hours, actual hours, sales, traffic, transactions, labor cost, wait time and NPS. Separate volume variance from execution variance.

## Control Evidence
Approved schedule; actual-vs-plan report; variance notes; coverage changes; weekly decision log.
