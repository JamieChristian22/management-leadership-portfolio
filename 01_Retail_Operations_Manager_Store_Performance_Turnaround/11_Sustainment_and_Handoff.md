# Sustainment & Handoff Plan

## Exit Criteria
- KPI targets met or credible recovery plan approved.
- All critical controls have BAU owners.
- SOPs are current.
- No unowned high risk.
- Dashboard refresh can occur without project-specific intervention.
- Open actions have owners/dates.

## Handoff Package
Operating workbook, SOPs, control log, RACI, decision log, data dictionary and final open-action list.

## 30-Day Check
Confirm controls remain active; review KPI drift; close residual project actions.

## 60-Day Check
Test whether owners are using escalation thresholds; review repeat exceptions and control burden.

## 90-Day Check
Confirm sustained trend, retire redundant controls, formalize remaining routines in standard store management cadence.

## Sustainability Rule
A control is not considered sustained because it exists on paper. It must have an owner, cadence, evidence, threshold and demonstrated use.
