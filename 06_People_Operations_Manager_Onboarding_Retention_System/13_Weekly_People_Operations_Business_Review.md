# Weekly People Operations Business Review

## 45-Minute Agenda
5 min overall health; 10 min upcoming-start readiness; 10 min cohort KPIs; 5 min manager/check-in exceptions; 5 min learning/readiness; 5 min feedback/risk themes; 5 min decisions/actions.

## Required Readout
What changed? Which process is failing? Which cohort needs support? What corrective action is underway? Who owns it? When is it due? What leadership decision is needed?

## Outputs
Action log, process risk register, SLA exceptions, learning actions, anonymized/aggregated employee-experience themes, and decisions.
