# Preboarding & Day-1 Readiness Standard

## Required Readiness
Signed documentation, HRIS record, payroll/tax setup, equipment, account/system access, security requirements, workspace/logistics, manager welcome plan, first-week calendar, training assignments, and support contacts.

## SLA
People Ops initiates readiness workflow at accepted offer. IT/equipment/access tasks due before start. Manager plan due before Day 1. Exceptions surfaced before start date.

## Readiness Gate
A hire is Day-1 Ready only when all critical prerequisites are complete or an approved contingency is documented.

## Control
Readiness dashboard reviewed for all starts 5 business days and 1 business day before start.
