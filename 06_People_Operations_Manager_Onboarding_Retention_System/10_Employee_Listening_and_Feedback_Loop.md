# Employee Listening & Feedback Loop

## Listening Points
End of Week 1, Day 30, Day 60, Day 90, plus open support channels.

## Themes
Role clarity, manager support, training, systems/access, workload, culture/connection, process friction, workplace support, and overall onboarding experience.

## Loop
Collect → protect/confidentialize as appropriate → categorize → quantify → review → assign action → communicate improvements → validate.

## Escalation
Safety, harassment/discrimination, payroll, accommodation, legal/compliance, or other sensitive issues follow designated HR/legal procedures rather than routine operational workflow.
