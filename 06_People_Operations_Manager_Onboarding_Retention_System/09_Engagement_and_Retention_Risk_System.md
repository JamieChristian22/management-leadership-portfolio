# Engagement & Retention-Risk System

## Signals
Onboarding completion, access/setup delays, missed manager check-ins, training gaps, role clarity, employee feedback, unresolved blockers, attendance at required onboarding events, and voluntary support requests.

## Risk Review
Signals are used to prioritize human review—not automate adverse employment decisions. Authorized People professionals validate context before intervention.

## Interventions
Manager coaching, role clarification, training support, workload review, workplace/resource support, relationship repair, process correction, or other appropriate People action.

## Privacy & Fairness
Use minimum necessary data, restrict access, document purpose, review for inconsistent application, and do not use protected characteristics as risk drivers.
