# Project Charter

## Sponsor
VP People / Head of HR

## Program Owner
People Operations Manager

## Scope
Preboarding, Day-1 readiness, onboarding milestones, manager accountability, training, 30/60/90-day check-ins, employee listening, retention-risk detection, cross-functional SLAs, KPI reporting, governance, controls, and handoff.

## Out of Scope
Compensation redesign, benefits redesign, performance-management replacement, HRIS replacement, workforce reduction decisions, and unrelated recruiting-process redesign.

## Success Criteria
Day-1 readiness >=96%; onboarding completion >=96%; training >=97%; 30-day manager check-ins >=95%; role readiness >=90%; satisfaction >=4.5; 90-day retention >=94%; high-risk population <=7%.

## Timeline
12-week optimization: W1–2 diagnose, W3–5 design, W6–9 pilot/iterate, W10–12 standardize/sustain.

## Governance
Weekly People Ops review; biweekly cross-functional readiness review; monthly leadership employee-experience review.
