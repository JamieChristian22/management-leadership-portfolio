# Sustainment & 30/60/90-Day Plan

## 30 Days
Audit readiness, onboarding task completion, manager check-ins, training exceptions, SLA performance, and data quality.

## 60 Days
Compare cohort role readiness, satisfaction, early retention, manager adherence, and recurring employee-experience themes.

## 90 Days
Validate sustained outcomes, automate low-risk workflow reminders, refresh standards, and transition the operating cadence to business-as-usual People Operations.

## Exit
Stable KPI performance, accepted owners, privacy-aware controls, repeatable reporting, and documented improvement governance.
