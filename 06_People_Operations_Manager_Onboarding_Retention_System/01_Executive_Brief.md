# Onboarding & Retention System — Executive Brief

## Executive Problem
Inconsistent onboarding creates avoidable delays, lower employee confidence, slower ramp-up, manager rework, and elevated early-tenure retention risk.

## Objective
Create a standardized People Operations system that makes new-hire readiness measurable, clarifies manager and cross-functional accountability, captures employee feedback, detects retention risk early, and establishes a repeatable 30/60/90-day experience.

## Baseline → Target
Day-1 readiness 71% → >=96%; onboarding completion 78% → >=96%; training 81% → >=97%; manager 30-day check-ins 66% → >=95%; 90-day role readiness 69% → >=90%; satisfaction 3.7 → >=4.5; 90-day retention 84% → >=94%; high-risk population 18% → <=7%.

## Strategic Priorities
1. Make Day-1 access, equipment, payroll, and manager preparation reliable.
2. Standardize onboarding milestones while preserving role-specific needs.
3. Create explicit manager accountability for 30/60/90-day coaching.
4. Measure role readiness and employee experience, not task completion alone.
5. Detect early retention risk through multiple signals and human review.
6. Connect employee feedback to process, manager, learning, and workplace improvements.

## Modeled Week-12 Outcome
Day-1 readiness 97%, onboarding completion 97%, training 98%, 30-day manager check-ins 96%, role readiness 92%, satisfaction 4.6/5, 90-day retention 95%, and high-risk population 6%.
