# People Operations Management — Onboarding & Retention System

A corporate-style simulated People Operations transformation focused on new-hire readiness, onboarding consistency, manager accountability, employee experience, early retention risk, role readiness, and sustainable 30/60/90-day governance.

## Business Scenario
New hires experience inconsistent onboarding, delayed system/equipment readiness, uneven manager follow-up, incomplete training, unclear role expectations, and limited early-tenure feedback. Leadership needs a standardized and measurable operating system that improves employee readiness while identifying retention risk early.

## Target Outcomes
- Day-1 readiness: 71% → >= 96%
- Onboarding completion: 78% → >= 96%
- Required training completion: 81% → >= 97%
- Manager 30-day check-in completion: 66% → >= 95%
- 90-day role readiness: 69% → >= 90%
- New-hire satisfaction: 3.7/5 → >= 4.5/5
- 90-day retention: 84% → >= 94%
- High retention-risk population: 18% → <= 7%

## Management Method
Prepare → Welcome → Enable → Coach → Listen → Detect Risk → Intervene → Validate → Sustain.

## Work Products
Executive brief, charter, employee-journey/root-cause analysis, target operating model, preboarding/Day-1 readiness standard, 30/60/90-day playbook, manager accountability model, training/readiness framework, engagement and retention-risk system, employee listening loop, HR/IT/payroll SLA, governance, risk/control plan, sustainment plan, data dictionary, decision log, modeled source data, and a formula-driven People Operations workbook.

> All employees, cohorts, metrics, survey results, and outcomes are simulated/modelled.
