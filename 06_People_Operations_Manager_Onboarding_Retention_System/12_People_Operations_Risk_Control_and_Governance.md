# People Operations Risk, Control & Governance

## Key Risks
Day-1 access failure, payroll setup issue, manager non-compliance, incomplete training, sensitive employee issue mishandling, inconsistent onboarding, privacy breach, unresolved early-tenure risk.

## Controls
Readiness gates, access controls, check-in tracking, training exceptions, documented escalation paths, privacy/need-to-know standards, recurring process audits.

## Governance
Weekly operating review; monthly leadership review; sensitive employee cases excluded from broad dashboards and handled through authorized case-management channels.
