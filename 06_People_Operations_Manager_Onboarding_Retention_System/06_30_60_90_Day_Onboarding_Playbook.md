# 30/60/90-Day Onboarding Playbook

## First 30 Days — Understand & Enable
Culture, role expectations, systems, required learning, relationships, initial work, weekly manager touchpoints, 30-day experience check.

## Days 31–60 — Practice & Develop
Role-specific execution, feedback, knowledge gaps, coaching, stakeholder integration, development actions, 60-day check-in.

## Days 61–90 — Demonstrate Readiness
Independent execution against role expectations, development plan, employee experience review, retention-risk review, 90-day readiness decision.

## Exit Standard
Required onboarding complete, critical training complete, role-readiness evidence documented, employee knows ongoing support channels, manager confirms next-stage expectations.
