# Target People Operations Operating Model

## Lifecycle
Offer Accepted → Preboarding Ready → Day-1 Ready → Week-1 Complete → 30-Day Check-In → 60-Day Development → 90-Day Role Readiness → BAU Employee Experience.

## Operating Principles
One accountable owner per milestone; employee data handled on a need-to-know basis; objective process measures separated from subjective manager judgment; risks reviewed by authorized People professionals; employee feedback leads to documented action.

## Daily/Weekly Controls
Upcoming-start readiness review, overdue onboarding task review, training exceptions, manager check-in exceptions, employee-support issues, and cross-functional SLA breaches.

## Monthly Controls
Cohort outcomes, retention trends, manager adherence, employee feedback themes, process equity/consistency, and improvement backlog.
