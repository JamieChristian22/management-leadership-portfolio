# Manager Accountability & Check-In Framework

## Manager Responsibilities
Pre-start welcome plan; Day-1 connection; weekly early-tenure check-ins; 30/60/90-day conversations; role clarity; feedback; training reinforcement; risk/escalation communication.

## Check-In Standard
Discuss role clarity, workload, relationships, support, training, blockers, confidence, development, and employee questions.

## Accountability
Completion tracked by cohort and manager. Repeated missed check-ins are escalated through People leadership using documented, consistent standards.

## Guardrail
Completion metrics do not replace the quality of the conversation; sensitive employee disclosures follow appropriate HR channels.
