# HR / IT / Payroll Cross-Functional SLA

## People Ops
Workflow launch, employee record, documentation, onboarding communications, readiness coordination.

## IT
Equipment, identity, system access, security enrollment, access exceptions.

## Payroll
Payroll profile, tax/direct-deposit readiness, pay-impacting issue resolution.

## Manager
Role plan, calendar, welcome, access approvals, training/coaching plan.

## SLA Governance
Exceptions logged with owner, business/employee impact, due date, workaround, and escalation. Recurring defects receive root-cause action.
