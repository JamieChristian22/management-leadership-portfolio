# Weekly Marketing Business Review

## 45-Minute Agenda
5 min executive health; 10 min channel economics; 10 min funnel/lead quality; 5 min Sales SLA; 5 min experiments; 5 min risks/actions; 5 min decisions.

## Required Readout
What changed? Why? Which investment should increase/decrease? What is the expected business impact? What is the risk? Who owns the next action? What decision is required?

## Outputs
Updated budget decision log, action plan, risk register, experiment log, and executive commentary.
