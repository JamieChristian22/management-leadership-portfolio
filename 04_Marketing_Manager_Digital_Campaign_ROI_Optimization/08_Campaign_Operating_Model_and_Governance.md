# Campaign Operating Model & Governance

## Daily
Spend pacing, tracking health, major anomalies, lead-routing failures, critical campaign issues.

## Weekly
Channel scorecard, funnel quality, experiments, budget shifts, creative fatigue, Sales feedback, risks/actions.

## Monthly
Executive investment review, budget portfolio, attribution confidence, pipeline/revenue, strategic experiments, and next-month allocation.

## Decision Standard
Every material change requires a hypothesis, owner, metric, guardrail, review date, and documented decision.
