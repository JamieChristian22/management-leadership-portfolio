# Budget Allocation & Investment Plan

## Allocation Principles
Fund proven profitable demand first; reserve controlled test budget; reallocate incrementally; assess marginal returns; avoid over-concentration; document each material budget decision.

## Weekly Reallocation Trigger
Review channels with ROAS below threshold, CPQL above target, low qualified-lead rate, weak downstream conversion, or material tracking issues.

## Decision Evidence
Spend, qualified pipeline, revenue, ROAS, CPQL, conversion, saturation, confidence, and operational capacity.

## Governance
Budget changes above the agreed threshold require Marketing leadership approval and Finance visibility.
