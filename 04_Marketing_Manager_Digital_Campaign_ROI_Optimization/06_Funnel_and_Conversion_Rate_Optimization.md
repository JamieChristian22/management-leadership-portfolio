# Funnel & Conversion Rate Optimization

## Funnel
Impression → Click → Landing Page → Lead → Qualified Lead → Opportunity → Customer.

## CRO Workstreams
Message/offer alignment, page speed, form friction, CTA clarity, social proof, audience-to-page relevance, retargeting, and nurture.

## Diagnosis
Separate traffic-quality problems from landing-page problems and sales-conversion problems.

## Guardrail
A conversion-rate increase is not considered successful if lead quality or downstream revenue deteriorates.
