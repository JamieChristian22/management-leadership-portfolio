# Lead Quality & Sales Alignment SLA

## Qualification
Define fit, intent, required fields, disqualification reasons, and routing logic.

## SLA
High-priority qualified leads routed immediately; Sales accepts/rejects within 1 business day; required follow-up completed within agreed SLA; rejection reason captured.

## Weekly Review
Lead volume, qualified rate, acceptance, follow-up SLA, opportunity conversion, rejection themes, and campaign/source quality.

## Escalation
Repeated SLA misses, unexplained rejection spikes, routing failures, or campaign-specific quality deterioration.
