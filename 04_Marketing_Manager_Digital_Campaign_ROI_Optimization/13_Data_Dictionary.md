# Data Dictionary

| Metric | Definition | Direction | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marketing ROI | (Attributed revenue - marketing cost) / marketing cost | Higher | Marketing Ops |
| ROAS | Attributed revenue / ad spend | Higher | Performance Marketing |
| CPQL | Marketing spend / qualified leads | Lower | Demand Gen |
| Qualified Lead Rate | Qualified leads / total leads | Higher | Marketing/Sales Ops |
| Lead-to-Customer % | Customers / leads | Higher | Revenue Ops |
| Landing Page CVR | Leads / landing-page sessions | Higher | Growth |
| Sales SLA % | Qualified leads followed up within SLA / qualified leads | Higher | Sales Ops |
| Wasted Spend % | Spend failing defined efficiency/quality threshold / total spend | Lower | Marketing Ops |
