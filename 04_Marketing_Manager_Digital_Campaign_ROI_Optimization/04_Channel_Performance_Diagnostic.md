# Channel Performance Diagnostic

## Diagnostic Questions
Which channels generate volume? Which generate qualified leads? Which convert to customers? Which produce revenue efficiently? Where is marginal spend deteriorating?

## Decision Framework
Scale: strong ROAS + quality + capacity.
Optimize: promising economics with fixable funnel friction.
Hold/Test: uncertain signal or insufficient sample.
Reduce/Pause: persistent weak economics after corrective testing.

## Guardrails
Do not scale based on CTR or CPL alone. Protect brand, compliance, audience saturation, lead quality, sales capacity, and measurement confidence.
