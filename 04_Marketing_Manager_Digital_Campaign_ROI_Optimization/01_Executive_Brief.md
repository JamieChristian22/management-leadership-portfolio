# Digital Campaign ROI Optimization — Executive Brief

## Executive Problem
Campaign volume is growing faster than marketing efficiency. Leadership can see spend and clicks, but cannot consistently connect channel investment to qualified pipeline, customers, and revenue.

## Objective
Build a performance-management system that reallocates budget toward profitable channels while improving lead quality, conversion, sales follow-up, and measurement discipline.

## Baseline → Target
ROI 128% → >=190%; ROAS 2.28x → >=3.10x; CPQL $186 → <=$125; qualified lead rate 38% → >=55%; lead-to-customer conversion 8.2% → >=12%; landing-page conversion 4.1% → >=6%; sales SLA 63% → >=92%; wasted spend 18% → <=7%.

## Strategic Priorities
1. Standardize attribution and campaign taxonomy.
2. Optimize to qualified pipeline/revenue, not traffic alone.
3. Reallocate spend using marginal performance and guardrails.
4. Improve landing-page and funnel conversion.
5. Tighten Marketing-to-Sales lead handling.
6. Establish test-and-learn and weekly investment governance.

## Modeled Week-12 Outcome
ROI 201%, ROAS 3.28x, CPQL $119, qualified lead rate 57%, lead-to-customer conversion 12.6%, landing-page conversion 6.3%, sales SLA 94%, and wasted spend 6%.
