# Sustainment & 30/60/90-Day Plan

## 30 Days
Audit campaign taxonomy, attribution completeness, budget controls, Sales SLA, and channel thresholds.

## 60 Days
Validate sustained ROI/ROAS, lead quality, conversion, creative fatigue controls, and experiment throughput.

## 90 Days
Rebaseline channel targets, automate repeatable controls, refresh budget portfolio, and transition optimization into business-as-usual governance.

## Exit
Stable economics, named owners, repeatable reporting, controlled budget changes, documented tests, and accepted governance.
