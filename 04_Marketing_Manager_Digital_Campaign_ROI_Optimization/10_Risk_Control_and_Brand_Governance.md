# Risk, Control & Brand Governance

## Key Risks
Attribution error, overspend, low-quality lead scaling, audience saturation, creative fatigue, Sales SLA failure, tracking outage, brand/compliance issue.

## Controls
Budget pacing, tracking QA, lead-quality thresholds, frequency review, creative rotation, SLA monitoring, approval workflow, anomaly alerts, weekly reconciliation.

## Escalation
Critical tracking loss, material overspend, brand/compliance issue, sudden quality deterioration, or revenue-impacting routing failure.
