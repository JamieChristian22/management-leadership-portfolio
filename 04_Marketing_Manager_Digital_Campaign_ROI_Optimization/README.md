# Marketing Management — Digital Campaign ROI Optimization

A corporate-style simulated performance marketing optimization work package focused on profitable growth, channel efficiency, lead quality, sales alignment, measurement governance, and disciplined budget allocation.

## Business Scenario
Marketing investment is spread across Paid Search, Paid Social, Display, Video, Email, and Content/Syndication. Leadership lacks a consistent view of which channels create qualified pipeline and revenue. High-volume channels are not always high-value channels, attribution is inconsistent, and budget decisions are made too slowly.

## Target Outcomes
- Marketing ROI: 128% → >= 190%
- ROAS: 2.28x → >= 3.10x
- Cost per qualified lead: $186 → <= $125
- Qualified lead rate: 38% → >= 55%
- Lead-to-customer conversion: 8.2% → >= 12.0%
- Landing-page conversion: 4.1% → >= 6.0%
- Sales follow-up within SLA: 63% → >= 92%
- Wasted / non-performing spend: 18% → <= 7%

## Management Method
Measure → Diagnose → Segment → Prioritize → Reallocate → Test → Validate → Scale → Govern.

## Work Products
Executive brief, charter, measurement/attribution framework, channel diagnostic, budget allocation model, funnel/CRO plan, lead-quality and sales-SLA plan, campaign governance, experimentation framework, risk/control plan, executive business review, sustainment plan, data dictionary, decision log, modeled source data, and a formula-driven marketing operating workbook.

> All companies, campaigns, budgets, customers, revenue, and results are simulated/modelled.
