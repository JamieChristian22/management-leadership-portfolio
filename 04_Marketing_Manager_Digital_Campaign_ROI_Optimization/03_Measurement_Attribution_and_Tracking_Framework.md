# Measurement, Attribution & Tracking Framework

## Measurement Hierarchy
Spend → impressions → clicks → landing-page conversions → leads → qualified leads → opportunities → customers → revenue.

## Required Tracking
Campaign/channel/source/medium, audience, creative, landing page, offer, spend, lead ID, qualification status, opportunity/customer outcome, revenue, and timestamp.

## Attribution Policy
Use one governed reporting model for executive decisions and document known limitations. Directional channel decisions must be supported by funnel quality and revenue, not last-click traffic alone.

## Data Quality Controls
UTM/taxonomy validation, duplicate lead checks, CRM source reconciliation, spend reconciliation, missing-revenue audit, and weekly anomaly review.
