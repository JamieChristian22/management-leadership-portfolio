# Project Charter

## Sponsor
VP Marketing

## Program Owner
Marketing Manager / Marketing Operations

## Scope
Paid and owned campaign performance, attribution, channel mix, budget allocation, funnel conversion, lead quality, Sales SLA, testing, reporting, risks, controls, and sustainment.

## Out of Scope
Brand redesign, pricing changes, sales compensation, core CRM replacement, and unrelated product roadmap changes.

## Success Criteria
ROI >=190%; ROAS >=3.10x; CPQL <=$125; qualified lead rate >=55%; lead-to-customer >=12%; landing-page conversion >=6%; sales follow-up SLA >=92%; wasted spend <=7%.

## Governance
Weekly campaign performance review; biweekly Marketing/Sales alignment review; monthly executive investment review.

## Timeline
12 weeks: W1–2 baseline/measurement, W3–5 optimization design, W6–9 experiments/reallocation, W10–12 scale/sustain.
