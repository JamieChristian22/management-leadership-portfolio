# Experimentation & Creative Testing Framework

## Test Record
Hypothesis, audience, channel, variable, primary KPI, guardrail, sample/duration, owner, result, decision.

## Example Tests
Landing-page headline, CTA/form length, audience segment, offer, creative concept, bidding strategy, nurture sequence.

## Rules
Predefine success; avoid changing multiple critical variables without design; review statistical and practical significance; preserve downstream quality guardrails; scale winners deliberately.
