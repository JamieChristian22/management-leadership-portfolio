# Data Dictionary

| Metric | Definition | Direction | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| OTIF % | Orders delivered on promised date and complete / eligible orders | Higher | Supply Chain |
| OTD % | Orders delivered on promised date / eligible orders | Higher | Supplier Mgmt |
| In-Full % | Orders delivered complete / eligible orders | Higher | Procurement |
| Lead-Time Variance | Absolute actual vs planned lead-time variance | Lower | Planning |
| Defect Rate % | Defective units/lots / received units/lots | Lower | Supplier Quality |
| Expedite Cost | Premium freight / recovery logistics cost | Lower | Supply Chain |
| Critical Stockouts | Critical-item stockout incidents | Lower | Planning |
| Corrective Action On-Time % | Supplier corrective actions closed by due date / due actions | Higher | Supplier Quality |
