# Weekly Supplier Performance Review

## 45-Minute Agenda
5 min overall health; 10 min OTIF/quality trends; 10 min red suppliers; 5 min stockouts/expedites; 5 min SCAR/CAPA; 5 min risks/actions; 5 min decisions.

## Required Readout
What failed? Why? What is contained? What corrective action is active? What is the business exposure? Who owns recovery? What decision/escalation is required?

## Outputs
Updated supplier scorecards, action plan, risk register, corrective-action log, expedite log, and decision log.
