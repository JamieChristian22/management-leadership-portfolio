# Risk, Escalation & Contingency Plan

## Key Risks
Capacity failure, chronic lateness, quality escape, logistics disruption, single-source dependency, forecast volatility, financial distress, and corrective-action failure.

## Escalation Triggers
Critical stockout risk; OTIF below red threshold; repeated quality defect; SCAR overdue >7 days; supplier misses committed recovery milestone; expedite cost exceeds tolerance.

## Contingencies
Alternate source, approved substitute, inventory reallocation, production prioritization, logistics alternative, executive supplier escalation, and sourcing event.
