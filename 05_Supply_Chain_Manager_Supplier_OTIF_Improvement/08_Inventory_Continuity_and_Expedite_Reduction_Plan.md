# Inventory Continuity & Expedite Reduction Plan

## Objective
Protect service while reducing reactive premium freight and excess buffers.

## Controls
Critical-item risk list, safety-stock review, lead-time accuracy, supplier commitment visibility, alternate-source assessment, expedite approval, and stockout root-cause review.

## Expedite Governance
Premium freight requires documented business risk, owner, cost, root cause, and prevention action. Repeated expedites trigger supplier/internal corrective action.

## Guardrail
Do not reduce inventory protection solely to improve working capital when supplier reliability remains unstable.
