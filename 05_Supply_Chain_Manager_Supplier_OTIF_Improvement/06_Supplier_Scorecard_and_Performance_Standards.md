# Supplier Scorecard & Performance Standards

## Scorecard
OTIF, OTD, In-Full, quality defect rate, lead-time variance, corrective-action closure, responsiveness, expedite events, and critical stockouts.

## Performance Bands
Green: meets/exceeds target.
Amber: within tolerance but requires recovery action.
Red: materially below threshold or critical business impact.

## Governance
Strategic suppliers reviewed monthly at minimum; red suppliers weekly until stable; trends and repeat causes retained for sourcing decisions.
