# Project Charter

## Sponsor
Director of Supply Chain

## Program Owner
Supply Chain Manager

## Scope
Supplier performance measurement, OTIF root causes, supplier segmentation, delivery/quality recovery, corrective actions, inventory continuity, procurement/S&OP governance, escalation, scorecards, and sustainment.

## Out of Scope
ERP replacement, warehouse redesign, product engineering changes unrelated to supplier defects, and major sourcing events outside approved escalation.

## Success Criteria
OTIF >=94%; OTD >=95%; In-Full >=97%; lead-time variance <=1.5 days; defect rate <=1.5%; expedite cost <=$30K/month; stockouts <=3/month; corrective-action closure >=92%.

## Timeline
12 weeks: W1–2 baseline/segmentation, W3–5 containment/root cause, W6–9 corrective action, W10–12 standardization/sustainment.

## Governance
Weekly supplier performance review; biweekly cross-functional operations review; monthly executive/sourcing review.
