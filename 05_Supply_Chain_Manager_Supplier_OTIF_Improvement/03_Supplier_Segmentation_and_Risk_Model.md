# Supplier Segmentation & Risk Model

## Segmentation Dimensions
Annual spend, material criticality, substitutability, lead time, quality history, OTIF, capacity risk, geographic/logistics exposure, and recovery time.

## Tiers
Strategic/Critical: high business impact; executive visibility and contingency plan.
Core: material operating importance; standard scorecard and monthly review.
Transactional: lower criticality; exception-based management.

## Risk Response
Higher-risk suppliers receive tighter review cadence, inventory protection, dual-source assessment, corrective-action governance, and defined escalation triggers.
