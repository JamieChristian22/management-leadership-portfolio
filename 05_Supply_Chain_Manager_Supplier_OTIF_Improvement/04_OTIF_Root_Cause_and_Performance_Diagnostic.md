# OTIF Root-Cause & Performance Diagnostic

## Failure Categories
Supplier capacity, production schedule, material shortage, logistics/carrier, forecast volatility, purchase-order accuracy, lead-time parameter error, quality hold, documentation/customs, and internal receiving issue.

## Diagnostic Method
Analyze OTIF at supplier, SKU/category, lane, plant/site, and reason-code level. Separate supplier-caused failures from internal planning/process failures.

## 5-Why Example
Late delivery → production completed late → constrained line capacity → unplanned priority changes → forecast/commitment process not aligned → capacity confirmation control absent.

## Priority
Address repeat high-impact causes before low-frequency exceptions.
