# Sustainment & 30/60/90-Day Plan

## 30 Days
Audit supplier scorecards, reason codes, recovery plans, lead-time parameters, and corrective-action aging.

## 60 Days
Validate sustained OTIF/quality, expedite reduction, inventory continuity, and red-supplier recovery.

## 90 Days
Rebaseline targets, automate repeatable exception reporting, incorporate performance into sourcing/business reviews, and confirm contingency coverage.

## Exit
Stable KPI performance, active owners, validated controls, repeatable governance, and accepted operational ownership.
