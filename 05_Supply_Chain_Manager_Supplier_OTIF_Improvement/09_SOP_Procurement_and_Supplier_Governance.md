# Procurement & Supplier Governance Operating Model

## Daily
Critical shortage review, overdue shipment exceptions, quality holds, and urgent supplier commitments.

## Weekly
Supplier scorecard exceptions, OTIF causes, SCAR aging, expedites, stockouts, actions/owners.

## Monthly
Strategic supplier business review, capacity/risk, sourcing decisions, performance trends, and commercial/accountability actions.

## Decision Standard
Material supplier decisions use verified performance, business criticality, recovery feasibility, and continuity risk.
