# Supplier Corrective Action (SCAR/CAPA) Process

## Workflow
Issue containment → evidence collection → root cause → corrective action → preventive action → owner/date → validation → closure.

## Closure Standard
Do not close on supplier promise alone. Require implementation evidence and sustained performance or validated effectiveness.

## Escalation
Overdue high-severity actions, repeated root cause, failed effectiveness check, or critical continuity risk escalate to Supply Chain leadership and Sourcing.
