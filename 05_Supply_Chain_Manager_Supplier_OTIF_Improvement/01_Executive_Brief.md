# Supplier OTIF Improvement — Executive Brief

## Executive Problem
Supplier delivery reliability is below operating requirements. Poor OTIF is driving expedites, stockouts, schedule instability, excess planner intervention, and inconsistent service.

## Objective
Build a supplier-performance operating system that improves delivery reliability and quality while protecting continuity and creating measurable supplier accountability.

## Baseline → Target
OTIF 76% → >=94%; OTD 82% → >=95%; In-Full 88% → >=97%; lead-time variance 4.8 → <=1.5 days; defects 3.6% → <=1.5%; expedite cost $84K → <=$30K/month; critical stockouts 12 → <=3/month; corrective-action on-time closure 58% → >=92%.

## Strategic Priorities
1. Segment suppliers by business criticality and performance risk.
2. Separate late-delivery, quantity, quality, and planning causes.
3. Establish supplier scorecards and weekly exception management.
4. Implement structured corrective actions with evidence-based closure.
5. Align inventory buffers and contingency plans to risk.
6. Tie sourcing/escalation decisions to sustained supplier performance.

## Modeled Week-12 Outcome
OTIF 95%, OTD 96%, In-Full 98%, lead-time variance 1.3 days, defects 1.3%, expedite cost $27K/month, stockouts 2/month, and corrective-action on-time closure 94%.
