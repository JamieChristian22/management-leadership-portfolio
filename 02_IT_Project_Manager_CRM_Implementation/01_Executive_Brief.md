# CRM Implementation — Executive Brief

## Executive Decision
Launch a controlled 10-week CRM implementation to replace fragmented spreadsheet-based pipeline management with a governed customer and opportunity workflow.

## Business Problem
Lead, opportunity, renewal, and follow-up data is maintained across disconnected spreadsheets owned by three teams. The current state creates duplicate records, inconsistent stage definitions, missed follow-ups, weak pipeline visibility, and manual reporting effort.

## Transformation Objectives
- Migrate 4,800 active customer and opportunity records with at least 98% migration accuracy.
- Achieve at least 90% active-user adoption by Week 10.
- Reduce duplicate-record rate from 11.0% to 3.0% or less.
- Reach at least 95% UAT pass rate before production release.
- Move management pipeline reporting from weekly manual refreshes to daily CRM reporting.
- Establish post-launch support, data governance, and adoption controls.

## Delivery Strategy
The implementation uses phased discovery, requirements confirmation, process design, data cleansing and mapping, configuration, integration validation, UAT, training, cutover, hypercare, and transition to steady-state ownership.

## Modeled Outcome
The scenario models a successful Week 10 release with 93% adoption, 99.1% migration accuracy, 2.7% duplicate rate, 97% UAT pass rate, 96% training completion, and daily pipeline reporting.

## Executive Recommendation
Approve transition from implementation governance to a 30-day hypercare period followed by monthly CRM governance focused on adoption, data quality, release controls, and business value realization.

> All results and data are simulated/modelled for demonstration purposes.
