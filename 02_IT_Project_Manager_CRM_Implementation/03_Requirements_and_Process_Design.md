# Requirements & Process Design

## Functional Requirements
1. Centralized account/contact/lead/opportunity records.
2. Standard opportunity stages with entry/exit criteria.
3. Required next-action and expected-close-date fields.
4. Renewal tracking and owner assignment.
5. Activity logging for calls, emails, meetings, and follow-ups.
6. Manager dashboards for pipeline, aging, conversion, and hygiene.
7. Duplicate detection and merge workflow.
8. Role-based permissions for sales, service, managers, and administrators.

## Non-Functional Requirements
Availability appropriate for business hours; least-privilege access; auditable changes; recoverable migration process; acceptable dashboard performance; documented support ownership.

## Future-State Workflow
Lead captured → ownership assigned → qualification → opportunity created → stage progression → next action maintained → close/renewal outcome → reporting and governance.

## Process Controls
Required fields, validation rules, stage exit criteria, stale-opportunity alerts, duplicate review queue, and weekly manager hygiene review.

## Acceptance
Requirements are baselined after business-owner and technical-owner sign-off. Changes after baseline enter formal change control.
