# Change Management & Training Plan

## Change Impacts
New system of record, standardized stages, mandatory data fields, changed reporting cadence, new manager hygiene responsibilities, and new support path.

## Stakeholder Segments
Executive sponsor, sales managers, frontline users, service users, IT/CRM administrators, reporting/analytics, and support.

## Enablement
Role-based training; sandbox practice; quick-reference procedures; office hours; manager reinforcement; launch communications; hypercare support.

## Adoption Measures
Training completion, weekly active users, required-field completion, stale opportunities, activity logging, support volume, and stakeholder pulse.

## Resistance Response
Identify cause, provide targeted coaching, remove workflow friction where justified, reinforce manager expectations, and escalate sustained non-adoption.
