# Project Charter

## Purpose
Redesign customer onboarding from closed-won through activation and first value.

## Sponsor
VP Customer Experience

## Workstream Owner
Product Operations

## In Scope
Customer segmentation, sales-to-CS handoff, onboarding milestones, setup/activation workflow, customer communications, support escalation, product feedback, KPI instrumentation, governance, and sustainment.

## Out of Scope
Core product replatforming, pricing redesign, enterprise contract terms, net-new headcount, and unrelated post-90-day lifecycle programs.

## Success Criteria
Completion >=92%; activation >=85%; TTV <=10 days; handoff >=95%; setup >=94%; CSAT >=4.5; escalations <=6%; retention risk <=8%.

## Governance
Weekly onboarding business review; biweekly sponsor review; monthly product/customer-success insight review.

## Timeline
12-week optimization: W1–2 diagnose, W3–5 redesign, W6–9 pilot/iterate, W10–12 standardize/sustain.
