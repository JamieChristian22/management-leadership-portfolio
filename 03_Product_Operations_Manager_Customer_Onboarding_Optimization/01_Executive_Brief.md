# Customer Onboarding Optimization — Executive Brief

## Executive Summary
Customer onboarding is underperforming because ownership, handoffs, setup expectations, and escalation paths are inconsistent. The result is slower time-to-value, lower activation, avoidable support volume, and elevated early retention risk.

## Objective
Create a measurable onboarding operating system that moves customers from closed-won to first value through standardized handoffs, milestone ownership, proactive risk detection, and weekly cross-functional governance.

## Baseline → Target
- Completion: 72% → >=92%
- 14-day activation: 61% → >=85%
- Median time-to-value: 18 → <=10 days
- Handoff completeness: 68% → >=95%
- Setup completion: 76% → >=94%
- CSAT: 3.8 → >=4.5
- Escalations: 14% → <=6%
- 30-day retention risk: 19% → <=8%

## Strategic Priorities
1. Make the sales-to-CS handoff complete and auditable.
2. Define milestone ownership and customer-facing expectations.
3. Use activation and time-to-value as operating metrics, not just completion.
4. Detect stalled accounts before they become escalations.
5. Convert support/VOC signals into product and process improvements.
6. Establish weekly governance and sustained controls.

## Modeled Week-12 Outcome
Completion 94%, activation 87%, median TTV 9 days, handoff completeness 97%, setup completion 95%, CSAT 4.6, escalation 5%, and early retention risk 7%.
