# Customer Journey & Root-Cause Analysis

## Current Journey
Closed-won → manual handoff → kickoff scheduling → setup → integration/configuration → training → first usage → first value → onboarding completion.

## Failure Points
- Missing use case, success criteria, technical contacts, or timeline at handoff.
- Kickoff delays due to unclear scheduling ownership.
- Setup tasks tracked across email/spreadsheets.
- Product usage is measured too late to detect stalled customers.
- Support tickets are handled separately from onboarding risk.
- Product feedback lacks a structured route to Product.

## Root-Cause Themes
Process ambiguity, ownership gaps, weak instrumentation, inconsistent customer communication, and reactive escalation.

## Prioritization
P1: Handoff completeness and kickoff speed.
P2: Setup/activation milestone visibility.
P3: Early-risk detection and escalation.
P4: VOC/support-to-product feedback loop.
