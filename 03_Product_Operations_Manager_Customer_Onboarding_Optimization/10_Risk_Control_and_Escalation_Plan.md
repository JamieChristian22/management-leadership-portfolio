# Risk, Control & Escalation Plan

## Key Risks
Incomplete handoffs; customer inactivity; integration blockers; training no-shows; unresolved support defects; unclear success criteria; excessive manual work; inconsistent milestone closure.

## Controls
Handoff validation, SLA monitoring, milestone aging, activation alerts, red-account review, support severity routing, completion evidence, weekly data-quality checks.

## Escalation Principle
Escalate based on customer/business impact and aging, not visibility or seniority.
