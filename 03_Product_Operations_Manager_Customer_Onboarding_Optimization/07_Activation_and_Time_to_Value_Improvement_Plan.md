# Activation & Time-to-Value Improvement Plan

## Activation Definition
Customer completes the product behaviors correlated with meaningful initial use, not merely account creation.

## Interventions
Segment onboarding by complexity; front-load prerequisites; schedule kickoff immediately; provide role-based setup checklists; trigger stalled-account alerts; offer guided office hours for at-risk cohorts.

## TTV Measurement
Track days from closed-won and kickoff to first-value event. Review median and 75th percentile by customer segment.

## Guardrail
Do not improve completion speed by skipping required setup, security, training, or success validation.
