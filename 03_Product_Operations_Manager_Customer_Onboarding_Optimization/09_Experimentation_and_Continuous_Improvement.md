# Experimentation & Continuous Improvement

## Experiment Template
Hypothesis, target cohort, intervention, primary metric, guardrail, duration, owner, result, decision.

## Example
Hypothesis: a pre-kickoff prerequisite checklist will reduce median setup delay.
Primary metric: days kickoff-to-setup.
Guardrail: CSAT and support tickets.
Decision: adopt only if improvement is sustained without increasing customer effort.

## Operating Rule
Experiments change one meaningful variable when possible and are documented before results are reviewed.
