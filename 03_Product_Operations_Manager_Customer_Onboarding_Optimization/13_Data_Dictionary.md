# Data Dictionary

| Metric | Definition | Direction | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onboarding Completion % | Customers meeting exit standard / eligible cohort | Higher | CS Ops |
| 14-Day Activation % | Customers activated within 14 days / eligible cohort | Higher | Product Ops |
| Median TTV | Median days from closed-won to first value | Lower | Product Ops |
| Handoff Completeness % | Accepted complete handoffs / total handoffs | Higher | Sales Ops |
| Setup Completion % | Customers completing required setup / cohort | Higher | CS Ops |
| CSAT | Average onboarding satisfaction score | Higher | CX |
| Escalation % | Customers with onboarding escalation / cohort | Lower | Support Ops |
| 30-Day Retention Risk % | Customers flagged high-risk at Day 30 / cohort | Lower | CS |
