# Target Onboarding Operating Model

## Lifecycle
Closed-Won → Handoff Accepted → Kickoff → Setup → Activation → First Value → Completion → 30-Day Health Check.

## Operating Principles
One accountable owner per milestone; one source of truth; explicit SLA/thresholds; customer expectations documented; risk surfaced early; product/support signals integrated.

## Daily Controls
New handoff review; stalled-account scan; overdue milestone review; escalation triage.

## Weekly Controls
Cohort KPI review; red-account review; support/VOC themes; experiment readout; owner/action/due-date review.

## Escalation
Critical setup blocker >1 business day; no kickoff within SLA; no activation by Day 10; executive customer risk; repeated product defect; missing customer owner; retention-risk score above threshold.
