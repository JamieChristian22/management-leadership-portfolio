# VOC, Support & Product Feedback Loop

## Inputs
Onboarding CSAT, support tickets, escalation reasons, call notes, setup blockers, feature requests, usage gaps, and churn/retention-risk reasons.

## Taxonomy
Process, education, configuration, integration, product defect, usability, missing capability, customer readiness, commercial expectation.

## Weekly Loop
Capture → categorize → quantify → assign owner → determine process/product response → communicate → verify impact.

## Product Escalation
Repeated blocker across multiple accounts, high-severity defect, high-volume usability friction, or issue materially affecting activation/TTV.
