# Sustainment & 30/60/90-Day Plan

## 30 Days
Audit SLA compliance, adoption of playbook, stalled-account detection, data quality, and owner adherence.

## 60 Days
Compare cohort activation/TTV, support burden, escalation themes, and product-feedback closure.

## 90 Days
Validate sustained outcomes, retire ineffective controls, prioritize automation, and incorporate proven onboarding standards into business-as-usual governance.

## Exit Criteria
Stable KPI performance, named owners, documented controls, repeatable reporting, and accepted operating ownership.
