# Weekly Onboarding Business Review

## 45-Minute Agenda
5 min overall health; 10 min KPI/cohort trends; 10 min red accounts and aging; 5 min support/VOC themes; 5 min experiments; 5 min actions/risks; 5 min decisions.

## Required Readout
What changed? Why? Which cohorts/accounts are at risk? What is the corrective action? Who owns it? When is it due? What decision is required?

## Outputs
Updated action log, risk log, decision log, experiment backlog, product feedback themes, and executive summary.
