# Sales-to-Customer-Success Handoff SLA

## Required Handoff Fields
Customer goals, primary use case, success metric, stakeholders, technical contact, purchased scope, integrations, commitments, risks, target go-live, renewal date, and known support needs.

## SLA
Sales submits within 4 business hours of closed-won. CS accepts/rejects within 1 business day. Rejected handoffs return with explicit missing fields.

## Quality Control
Weekly completeness audit; rejection reasons categorized; repeat defects coached with sales leadership.

## Success Measure
>=95% complete handoffs and <5% rejection rate.
