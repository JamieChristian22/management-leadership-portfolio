# Management Operating System

> **Simulated work sample:** All events, people, approvals, records, costs, and outcomes below are fictional portfolio exercises. They are not employer results or evidence of a live system implementation.

## Cadence with a decision purpose

| Cadence | Participants | Inputs | Decisions / outputs | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Daily, 15 minutes during critical delivery | Delivery and support leads | Blockers, incidents, next commitments | Immediate owner and escalation | Delivery Lead |
| Weekly, 30 minutes | Functional owners | KPI variance, capacity, aged actions | Priorities, recovery actions and resource changes | Operations Lead |
| Biweekly, 30 minutes | Manager and employee | Workload, feedback and development goals | Support, clarified expectations and next check-in | People Manager |
| Monthly, 45 minutes | Sponsor and accountable owners | Benefits, costs, risks and quality | Continue, stop, expand or reprioritize | Sponsor |

Cancel or shorten a meeting when no decision or coordination need exists. Distribute evidence beforehand; keep the meeting record focused on decisions.

## Weekly executive update example

**Status: amber.** Delivery preparation is progressing, but an unresolved access mapping dependency may move acceptance by two days.

**Evidence:** three of four handoff commitments met on time; the fourth was renegotiated before deadline. Eight hours of contingency remain after optional reporting was deferred.

**Decision needed:** Sales Ops to assign a mapping owner by Tuesday noon. If unassigned, sponsor chooses a reduced scope or revised date.

**Actions:** Analyst updates the blocker log daily; Support Lead verifies the corrected contact field; Delivery Lead reports decision impact Wednesday.

All figures refer to the leadership exercise, not a real project.

## Priority method

First handle immediate safety, security or service-critical issues through the applicable process. For remaining work, compare business value, urgency, effort, dependencies and reversibility. Record why work is deferred; a numeric score is a discussion aid, not automatic authority.

## Action standard

Every action has a stable ID, accountable owner, due date, status, blocker and completion evidence. An overdue item is reviewed for cause and impact, not silently reset. A closed item must show the intended output; “email sent” is insufficient if the action was “obtain acceptance.”

## Decision record standard

Record the problem, alternatives, assumptions, chosen option, expected benefit, cost/risk, approver, review date and reversal trigger. Preserve earlier assumptions when updating the record so lessons can be traced.

## 30/60/90-day entry plan

| Period | Focus | Deliverables | Success check |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Learn the process and evidence | Stakeholder map, KPI definitions, workflow observations | Owners agree what each metric means |
| Days 31–60 | Test one bounded improvement | Pilot plan, baseline, guardrails, weekly review | Decision based on mature observations |
| Days 61–90 | Standardize or revise | Handoff, controls, training, benefit review | Named owners can sustain the process |

This is an adaptable entry plan. It is not a promise that unfamiliar operations can be transformed in 90 days.
